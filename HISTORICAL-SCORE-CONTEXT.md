# Historical optical counterexample

The prior SCORE analysis found approximately +4.074 mag for BB8 minus BB10 and +4.098 mag for BB9 minus BB10 on August 31, 2026, after range normalization and source/date/phase matching. These are pass-level summaries of three and four unique observation pairs. The source raw responses for all three catalog identities are included in data/.

This context is retained because it argues against any universal brightness threshold for incomplete deployment. It is an inherited analysis result, not regenerated by reproduce.py. Full original scripts and derived outputs remain in the private working archive and can be adapted for a subsequent public reproduction release. Do not treat the matches as independent satellites or assume their photometric band is calibrated to Slingshot MAG.
