## CelesTrak

Orbital records are sourced from CelesTrak, with standard GP derived from US Space Surveillance Network tracking and AST supplemental data derived from operator ephemerides. Retain provenance and consult https://celestrak.org/usage-policy.php before automated retrieval or redistribution. No blanket third-party license is granted by this package.

## Research transformations

This independent analysis applies range normalization, source/geometry matching and descriptive orbital-proxy comparisons. It is not endorsed by the source organizations. Original observer ORCID identifiers remain in the raw SCORE JSON and matched outputs. No purchased Slingshot raw data are included.
